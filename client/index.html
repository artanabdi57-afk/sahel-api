Shop Management Software for Somalia

Sahel is shop management software built for Somalia. Every day, shop owners across Mogadishu, Hargeisa, and Kismayo use Sahel to track inventory, record sales, and manage customer credit — without needing a steady internet connection. Sahel speaks Somali, works through intermittent connectivity, and understands how Somali businesses actually operate.

What Sahel does

Sahel records every sale, alerts you when stock is low, tracks customer debts, and manages your expenses — all from your phone, in Somali, English, and Arabic.
